01 — About

Background

I am a researcher at the Korea Research Institute for Vocational Education and Training (KRIVET), where I focus on vocational education policy, National Competency Standards (NCS) curriculum development, and quantitative research methodologies.

My work aims to bridge evidence-based research with practical policy implications, contributing to a more effective and equitable vocational education and training system in Korea.

I also teach a graduate seminar on Education and Humanity at Chungbuk National University, where I engage students — including international students — in exploring the philosophical and social dimensions of education.
